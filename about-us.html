About Our Health Center

The Barangay Health Center provides basic health services and community health programs for residents.

Our online portal helps residents access health information, view records, request appointments and communicate feedback to the health center.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible and community-oriented basic health services to residents.

👁️ Our Vision

A healthy, informed and active community where residents can access essential health services.

🩺 Our Services

• Health Consultation

• Immunization

• Maternal Health

• Child Health

• Community Health Programs

• Health Records
